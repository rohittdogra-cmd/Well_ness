# Regional wellbeing section

Reviewed 6 November 2026. These are published estimates, not live counters.

- India: NMHS 2015–16, nearly 150 million people needing active intervention. Government of India, 3 December 2018: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1554374
- India: 10.6% adult (18+) mental-disorder prevalence in NMHS. Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Parliament reply, 2 February 2021: https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/253/A114.pdf?source=pqars
- India survey coverage: 12 states in six regions, during 2015–16. NIMHANS: https://indianmhs.nimhans.ac.in/phase1/detailed-news.php?eid=6
- WHO South-East Asia: 289 million people with a mental, neurological or substance-use condition, as reported in WHO's 24 July 2025 dashboard announcement: https://www.who.int/southeastasia/news/detail/24-07-2025-who-south-east-asia-launches-mental-health-dashboard-to-accelerate-and-strengthen-evidence-based-action

The India and WHO figures have different condition coverage, age/population scopes and reference periods. Do not add, rank or directly compare them. The WHO announcement publication date is not a 2025 measurement period. The regional selector is specifically WHO South-East Asia, not all of Asia. The source page's membership list and current WHO geography exclude Indonesia, which transferred regions in May 2025. The map highlights geography only; it does not assign a national prevalence or intensity to any country. The Maldives, absent at 110m resolution, is represented by a small geographic marker.
